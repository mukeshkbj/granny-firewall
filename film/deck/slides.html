The problem
$4.9B

lost by Americans over 60 to fraud in 2024 — FBI IC3

The phone is still the scammers' favorite door. Every con follows the same script: authority → urgency → secrecy → gift cards.

Why nothing works

"Just don't answer unknown numbers" doesn't survive real life.

The pharmacy calls. The doctor's office calls. The plumber calls from a cell phone. Someone has to pick up.

What it does

Every call hits the wire first.

Unknown callers never ring grandma's phone. The screener answers — "Hello, you've reached the Miller residence, who's calling?" — and scores the conversation live across seven scam categories. Every marker lands on the dashboard mid-sentence, exact quote attached, while a risk dial climbs.

Then the call forks three ways
PASS

Legitimate

Pharmacy, neighbor, real friend — passed through. Claiming to be family? Say the codeword.

SCREEN

Suspicious

The screener keeps asking questions while the family gets a quiet alert.

STALL

Clearly a scam

Instead of hanging up — the firewall flips into a confused 78-year-old and wastes the scammer's time instead.

The honeypot

Every minute a scammer spends coaxing card numbers out of "Ethel"
is a minute they're not calling an actual victim.

When it ends, the family gets a threat report: who the caller claimed to be, what they asked for, how much money they wanted, the red flags that fired, and what to do next.

Built on AssemblyAI

Voice Agent API

Runs the live call — STT, reasoning, voice, turn-taking and tool calls over one WebSocket. The tools are the product: flag_marker · verify_codeword · pass_to_family · set_persona · whisper_alert · end_call

Universal-Streaming

Powers Analyze mode — feed any recorded call through the pipeline and watch markers land as the transcript streams.

LLM Gateway

Per-turn classification on the live stream, plus the structured verdict that closes the post-call report.

Speech Understanding

Async transcription with speaker labels, entities, sentiment and PII redaction fills in what was actually said.
